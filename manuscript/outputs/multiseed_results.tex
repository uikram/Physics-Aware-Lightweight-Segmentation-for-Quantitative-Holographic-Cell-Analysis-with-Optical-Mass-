{\Large\bfseries Multi-seed results}\\[2pt]{\small 32-bit phase data, LoRA rank, loss and seed}\par\vspace{4pt}

Results of 180 LoRA training runs: 3 models (EdgeSAM, MobileSAM, MobileNet-UNet), 5 ranks (2, 4, 8, 16, 32), 4 loss settings and 3 seeds. Source: \texttt{results.json}, generated 2026-10-27T03:13:19.329485+00:00. Class names follow the manuscript order: mask value 1 = discocyte, mask value 2 = spherocyte, mask value 3 = echinocyte, mask value 4 = stomatocyte.

\section*{How to read the numbers}
\begin{description}\itemsep1pt
\item[Dice] overlap between predicted and annotated cells, averaged over the four cell classes. 1 is perfect, higher is better.
\item[AJI, BF1] AJI: how well single cells are separated. BF1: how well the cell outlines match. Both 0 to 1, higher is better.
\item[IPE] error of the integrated phase (the quantity that gives the dry mass), in percent. Lower is better.
\item[Lost class] a run in which at least one cell class has Dice below 0.05, so that class is practically not found. Such runs are kept in every mean. A small superscript number next to a value is the number of runs, out of 3 seeds, that lost a class.
\item[Seeds] each setting was trained 3 times with different random seeds (42, 43, 44). $\pm$ is the standard deviation over these seeds. With three seeds the numbers describe what happened; they are not significance tests.
\item[Setting] 32-bit phase maps in radians, last-epoch model, 66 validation images. The rank is the LoRA rank (2 to 32). Losses: Dice only, +PMC, +PMC+BGA, Full (+IPP).
\end{description}

\section*{Key points}

\begin{itemize}\itemsep3pt
\item \textbf{EdgeSAM.} With the full objective, the mean Dice over seeds is highest at rank 32 (0.738) and lowest at rank 2 (0.625). 3 of 15 full-objective runs lost a class (at rank 2, 8, 16); over all four losses 11 of 60 runs did. Classes lost most often: spherocyte (11). The full objective has a lower global IPE than Dice only in 15 of 15 seed-by-seed comparisons (same rank and seed).
\item \textbf{MobileSAM.} With the full objective, the mean Dice over seeds is highest at rank 16 (0.813) and lowest at rank 32 (0.687). 1 of 15 full-objective runs lost a class (at rank 32); over all four losses 6 of 60 runs did. Classes lost most often: spherocyte (6), stomatocyte (1). The full objective has a lower global IPE than Dice only in 14 of 15 seed-by-seed comparisons (same rank and seed).
\item \textbf{MobileNet-UNet.} With the full objective, the mean Dice over seeds is highest at rank 32 (0.476) and lowest at rank 4 (0.406). 15 of 15 full-objective runs lost a class (at rank 2, 4, 8, 16, 32); over all four losses 56 of 60 runs did. Classes lost most often: spherocyte (54), stomatocyte (43), echinocyte (10). The full objective has a lower global IPE than Dice only in 13 of 15 seed-by-seed comparisons (same rank and seed).
\end{itemize}

\section*{Scope and conventions}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
\item Source: \texttt{results.json} (generated 2026-10-27T03:13:19.329485+00:00). 180 LoRA runs: 3 architectures $\times$ 5 ranks $\times$ 4 losses $\times$ 3 seeds (42, 43, 44).
\item Checkpoint: last epoch. Validation images: 66. Input: 32-bit phase maps in radians (no 8-bit conversion).
\item Losses: Dice only, +PMC, +PMC+BGA, Full (+IPP). Ranks: 2, 4, 8, 16, 32.
\item A run \emph{lost a class} when the Dice of any class is below 0.05. Such runs are counted in every mean, none is excluded.
\item Mean $\pm$ SD over seeds (sample SD, $n$ as stated). IPE is the global integrated phase error in percent. Per-class columns follow the mask-value order: discocyte, spherocyte, echinocyte, stomatocyte (mask values 1 = discocyte, 2 = spherocyte, 3 = echinocyte, 4 = stomatocyte).
\item Paired differences are computed seed by seed (same seed in both runs), with a 95\% $t$-interval; with three seeds the interval is wide and is given for description only, not as a significance test.
\end{itemize}
